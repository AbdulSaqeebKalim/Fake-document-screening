      02 — Technical approach

      What's under the pipeline

      Five technology components mapped to a four-stage processing sequence.

      Technologies used
      01

      Transformer OCR

      LayoutLMv3 & TrOCR for low-latency multilingual text extraction.

      02

      Vision AI

      Dual-branch ResNet + Error Level Analysis (ELA) CNNs for tampering detection.

      03

      Biometrics

      ArcFace / MagFace angular-margin facial embedding, sub-0.001% FAR.

      04

      Deployment

      Edge ONNX models, ICAO e-Gate and optical scanner integration.

      05

      Decisioning

      Bayesian inference engine for risk score fusion.

      Methodology & process
      S1

      Preprocessing

      Adaptive binarization, perspective rectification, security-layer slicing.

      S2

      Vision AI models

      Transformer OCR + noise/ELA analysis + dual-branch ResNet.

      S3

      Biometric matcher

      ArcFace/MagFace embedding + passive anti-spoofing.

      S4

      Risk aggregator

      Bayesian fusion + traffic-light scoring + explainable heatmaps.

      03 — Feasibility & viability

      Why this is buildable at checkpoint scale

      Technical

      Integrates natively with ICAO e-Gates & optical scanners; edge ONNX models run locally in under 2.5 seconds without cloud dependency.

      Operational

      Zero disruption to inspection lanes; intuitive visual overlays require no specialized officer training.

      Economic

      Cuts processing time by 80%, reducing officer overtime costs while doubling lane capacity with no new physical infrastructure.

      Legal & privacy

      Privacy-by-design — strict volatile-memory execution (no stored biometrics), full data-sovereignty compliance, tamper-proof audit trails.

      Varied document formats & languages across issuing countries
      →
      Layout-robust transformer OCR with multilingual, multi-script training
      Sophisticated spoofing — masks, screens, printed photos
      →
      3D passive liveness detection combined with depth & reflection analysis
      Real-time watchlist / database dependency
      →
      Cached local lookups with asynchronous sync to INTERPOL / national feeds

      04 — Impact & benefits

      What changes for officers and travelers

      Speed

      Reduces total identity & document screening time from 3+ minutes down to under 3 seconds per passenger.

      Consistency

      Eliminates subjective human variance and officer fatigue across airports, land borders, and sea ports.

      Accuracy

      Catches sub-pixel digital alterations and lookalike impersonators that bypass naked-eye review.

      Security

      Standardized, explainable decisions and a forensic, time-stamped audit trail for intelligence and legal prosecution.

      Economic

      Doubles checkpoint lane capacity and cuts officer overtime costs without new physical infrastructure.

      Social

      Strengthens national border security while keeping the passenger experience fast and frictionless.

      Privacy

      No biometric data is stored — volatile in-memory processing supports data-sovereignty compliance.
